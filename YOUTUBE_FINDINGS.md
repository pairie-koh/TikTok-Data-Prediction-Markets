# YouTube Findings

How are prediction markets actually entering political discourse?

Following our TikTok analysis, we scraped ~4,078 YouTube videos discussing prediction markets in US politics. Some initial findings:

- YouTube has prediction market content going back to **2007** — a video arguing for regulating political betting before most people knew prediction markets existed. In 2008, ABC News 20/20 ran a segment on InTrade called "Win Big Bucks Betting on Politics."

- YouTube has real **down-ballot coverage** — 20% of videos cover Senate/House/Governor races. Entire channels like @ElectionPredictionsOfficial do race-by-race prediction market based forecasts. TikTok prediction market content is almost entirely presidential.

- One of the ways that prediction markets entered mainstream political discourse was through **crypto YouTube** — crypto YouTubers like @HamsterKombat covered Polymarket as crypto news, which exposed millions of viewers to US elections who never sought out political content. Nearly a quarter of all YouTube prediction market political videos come through this crypto pipeline.

We're digging deeper into this dataset — stay tuned for more findings on how prediction market odds are being framed, who the key creators are, and how the conversation differs across platforms.
