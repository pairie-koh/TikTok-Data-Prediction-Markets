The U.S. will strike Iran by the... More than 500 million dollars have been bet on this kind of contract on the PolyMarket platform before the strikes on Saturday. Problem, the American law prohibits betting on war, murders or other violent events. The timing is also at the heart of the controversy. 6 bettors identified by an analysis company have each made more than 1 million dollars in profits on these contracts, betting on the exact date of the strikes. A situation reminiscent of the bets around the departure of Nicolas Maduro. An anonymous person had then bet more than 400,000 dollars, betting just before his capture, raising suspicions of criminal offences. After the start of the strikes and the death of Ramenei, the contracts were closed and some users were reimbursed to try to put out the fire. But the controversy continues and several Democratic parliamentarians push to ban this kind of platform. On the side of PolyMarket and Kalshi, all speculation is denied, aiming to take advantage of death.
