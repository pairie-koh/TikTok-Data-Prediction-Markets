This is how you get all the Chaos Badges. For this one you need to get 3 wins in a row in Chaos Mode, which is the easy one and I'd recommend this deck here. For this one you must take down the Crown Tower without damaging any Princess Towers on their side, you can use this deck here and just hope you will take their King Tower before they take yours. For the last one you must get 4 modifiers, one being the Barbarian Launcher Mortar, the second being the Party Rocket, the third being the Party Goblin Hut and the last being the Wizard Trio. You have to get them all in one game and you don't have to win. Here's a deck I recommend to complete it, if you've done it already post your deck in the comments. This is a very luck based badge, I've heard of some people doing over 50 games and still not getting the right modifiers, let me know if you get it done since it will probably be one of the most rare badges of all time. Remember to leave your deck in the comments, if there's any other badges you find, let me know and remember to follow.
