Welcome to the pre-market flyby for Wednesday, March 11th. U.S. stock futures were subdued in early choppy trading as investors assessed the cross-currents of crude oil prices and the upcoming key inflation report. Oracle is up 9% after the software giant's upbeat revenue forecast calmed worries over faster returns from its spending on artificial intelligence infrastructure. Campbell's is down 5% after the company said their Q2 results fell short of expectations and lowered its full-year outlook to reflect a more cautious view for the balance of the year. Unifirst is up 9% after Syntas agreed to acquire the company for $310 per share in cash and stock. Aerovironment has dropped 10% after the drone maker cut its revenue guidance for the full year. Nike is up 2% after Barclays upgraded their rating on the stock to a buy, citing recent operational progress and management's disciplined actions.
