PoliMark is the international betting-based prediction platform. In the last few hours, it ranked Iván Cepeda as the favorite to win the 2026 presidency. The data generated debate because, although this platform is not a scientific survey, it reflects how certain global markets are seeing the Colombian political panorama. Iván Cepeda's feedback coincides with the unity of the historical pact around his candidacy and the fall of the image of the national government. Paradoxically, it has strengthened the ideological sector of the coalition. However, experts warn that PoliMark does not measure the intention of voting. It measures perceptions, speculation, and bets of users outside the reality of average voters in Colombia. What is worrying and what cannot be ignored is that this type of headline creates a symbolic effect. It instills in the conversation the idea that the left has a clear path to repeat or deepen its political project. That's where you have to be alert. Let's remember that we must not underestimate the left. They play the narrative well, move chips in silence, and take advantage of any symbolic advantage to mark a trend. Prediction is not election.
