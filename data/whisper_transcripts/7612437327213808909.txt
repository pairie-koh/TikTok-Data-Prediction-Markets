Forget the pundits and the polls. If you want to find out what actually happened at Trump's State of the Union, follow the money. We just saw over $17 million wagered on Trump's speech across Cali-Sheehan Polymarket. This wasn't just a speech, it was a big money parlay. If you bet the over for duration of the speech, you had a very good night. Markets priced a 78% chance the speech would break 100 minutes. Trump didn't just break it, he shattered the record. One hour and 47 minutes. That officially beats Bill Clinton's year 2000 record by almost 20 minutes. Then there were the word mention bets. Because the US men's team just took Olympic gold, hockey was an 89% favorite for a mention. Trump gave the shout out, bidders cashed out. Now let's get weird. One of the most active markets? Whether Trump would drink water. 81% said no shot. They were right, not a single sip. And for the long shot hunters, someone bet $62,000 on whether Trump would say the word discombobulator. He didn't. Sorry to that yes holder. Meanwhile, viewership tanked to 27.8 million, just like the markets predicted.
