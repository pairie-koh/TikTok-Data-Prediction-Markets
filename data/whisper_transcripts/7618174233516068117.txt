Humanoid robots were deployed to the front line in Ukraine last month, a massive escalation in robotic warfare and a glimpse at just how crazy the future could be. The units sent were Foundation Phantom Mark 1 Humanoid Robots, a 5 foot 9, 180 pound humanoid robot designed specifically to handle human-centric environments, meaning it can open doors, climb stairs and use the same tools and weapons that soldiers do, including holding handguns, rifles and shotguns.
