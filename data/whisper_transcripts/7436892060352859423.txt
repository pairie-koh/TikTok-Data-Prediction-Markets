The FBI conducted a raid on the Manhattan apartment of Shane Coplin, CEO of Polly Market, a leading prediction betting platform. The raid took place early Wednesday morning, just a week after Polly Market accurately predicted Donald Trump's victory in the 2024 presidential election. FBI agents seized Coplin's phone and other electronics, sparking accusations of political retaliation. Polly Market, which operates outside the U.S. due to regulatory restrictions, has long faced scrutiny. The platform previously paid a $1.4 million fine for failing to register with the Commodity Futures Trading Commission. However, it gained new attention during the election, as its market showed Trump with a 58.6 percent chance of winning ahead of Election Day. Critics argue the raid is part of a broader effort to discredit Polly Market, with a company spokesperson labeling it as political retribution. The FBI has not provided details on the investigation, and Coplin has not been charged. Meanwhile, Polly Market's future in the U.S. hangs in the balance as it navigates increasing regulatory and political pressure.
