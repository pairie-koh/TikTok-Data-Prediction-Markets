This was, without a doubt, the best steakhouse I've ever been to in San Francisco. This is Harris's. You've probably seen them off of Van Ness. They've been around since the 80s. But let's dive right into it. We got the crispy crab cakes to start. I love crab cakes that have a lot of crab and not just filler. Texture and the flavor were just perfection. Up next was the steak tartare, which was so unbelievably flavorful. There were capers in it, came with a little side salad, and these little crispy toasts. And of course, the main star of the show, the bone-in ribeye that came with table-side baked potato service. Like, are you kidding me? Look at all those fixins. Every single steak comes with a side of veggies and your choice of potato. Like, that is how a steak should be cooked. That is perfection to me. Executed it so well. Favorite side dish at any steakhouse is the creamed spinach. Theirs was so unbelievably good, I couldn't stop reaching for it. It doesn't stop there. We had to get dessert, of course. The rice pudding with this toffee brittle on top to the key lime cheesecake, baked Alaska, and carrot cake with ice cream. Oh my gosh. Like, we just couldn't stop eating. It, like, hurt at this point. Everything was so delicious. But what really set them apart was their service. Everyone there was just so kind, so nice. It's family-owned and ran. The whole experience was a 10 out of 10 from start to finish. You have to try.
