Intercontinental Exchange, New York Stock Exchange owners, will invest $2,000,000,000 in PolyMarket. What is PolyMarket? PolyMarket is a platform that is basically a betting house where people can invest or bet in real life events, from sports betting to, for example, how many tweets Elon Musk will make in a week. How does it work? PolyMarket is not a regular betting house. The fees are not set arbitrarily as a house, but are basically determined by the interaction of supply and demand. Each stock costs between $0 and $1 depending on the probabilities, and if you get it right, they pay you $1. So your profit is basically the difference between 1 minus the probability for which you bet. So if you bet on an event where the probability that it happens is 87%, that 0.87 is what the stock costs you, and they pay you $1. So the difference of 3 cents is going to be your profit. But how does PolyMarket make money? They charge 2% of each transaction earned, and also when someone loses, they don't get paid anything, they get paid $0. PolyMarket works with cryptocurrencies, and this is an important milestone for the industry. In fact, after this investment, the investment of ICE, PolyMarket has reached the value of $8,000,000,000. And now, with the support of the company that owns the New York Stock Exchange, PolyMarket ceases to be a simple betting house, and begins to become a market where probabilities are what are traded.
