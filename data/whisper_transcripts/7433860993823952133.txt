What is PolyMarket? PolyMarket is a platform in Paris where users can create prediction markets by asking specific questions about future events. People can buy and sell shares in future results such as political elections, sports events, or economic development, and a little bit of everything. These shares represent a probability that the event will happen or not. Currently, we find a lot of Paris on the platform that concerns American elections. But there are also other Paris that are a little bit crazier. For example, is Elon Musk going to do a podcast with the Octa Girl? Or is Trump going to say what he said before the elections? So, obviously, people can bet on a little bit of any event. And all of this is a decentralized market that is based on the Polygon blockchain. In fact, PolyMarket has become very popular. It allows us to use a little bit of common knowledge, if you will, the wisdom of the crowd to try to predict the outcome of an event. There are some people who even go so far as to say that the results on PolyMarket can be considered to be more reliable than, for example, polls for several reasons. First of all, the participants use their own money to speculate on the results of an event, unlike people who answer a poll and who don't really have a consequence on what they choose. Then, there are more people who speak on PolyMarket than in most polls, which leads to what is called crowd wisdom, a phenomenon where the predictions of several people often turn out to be more true. Then, PolyMarket adjusts constantly according to developments. For example, if something happens with Rump or Kamala, people will react in real time on prediction markets like PolyMarket, unlike a poll that captures the opinion at one precise moment. Finally, PolyMarket is accessible to everyone, so it helps to reduce the sampling cost a little bit.
