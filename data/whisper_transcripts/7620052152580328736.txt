Here is the participation rate at noon for this second round of municipal elections. It was 20.33% of the registrants, according to the Ministry of the Interior. In comparison, the participation rate at the same time was 19.37% in the first round, and during the 2020 municipal elections, the participation rate in the second round at noon was 15.29%. It is therefore a 5-point progression compared to 2020. This year, the election was marked by the pandemic and the fear of COVID-19, which led to abstention. So, if you too want to slip into the urn, a little reminder, the polling stations will close their doors at 6 p.m. in a large majority of municipalities, at 7 p.m. or 8 p.m. in the largest cities.
