For the prediction market, two well-known and biggest names are Kalshi and Polymarket. But big players like CME Group, Robinhood, and Coinbase are also competing to offer prediction market products. Polymarket's own crypto strategy is wrapped in rigid regulations and building maximum liquidity. They relaunched again to the United States with CFTC license and Web2 expansion via UFC, NFL, and Yahoo Finance. Kalshi's strategy utilizes the legal clarity they already have and order book liquidity to expand globally to 140 countries and on-chain integration with names like Solana, Jupiter, and Phantom.
