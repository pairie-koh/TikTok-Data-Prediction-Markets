A historian who has correctly predicted nearly every presidential winner in the last 40 years has revealed his pick for the U.S. election, and it is Kamala Harris. Alan Lickman has correctly predicted every presidential election winner since 1984, excluding 2000's George Bush versus Al Gore, but we all know how that one went. He was also one of the few political analysts to accurately predict that Trump would beat Hillary Clinton in 2016. Lickman ignores polls and pundits and instead uses his own system based on what he calls the 13 keys to the White House, some of which include charisma, foreign policy, scandals, and social unrest. It's a series of 13 true or false statements that focuses on the incumbent president's party, in this case, Harris' Democrats. If six or more of the statements are false, then the challenger, in this case Trump, is predicted to win. But as of now, Lickman says that only three of those statements are false, which means that Harris wins. Lickman is confident in his predictions, saying the keys absolutely will work.
