The last time North Carolina sent a Democrat to the U.S. Senate, Jay-Z and Beyoncé had just gotten hitched and Heath Ledger was playing the Joker. So it's been a minute. But recent polling suggests big things could be on the horizon for state Democrats. In the upcoming Senate race, former Democratic Governor Roy Cooper is leading GOP frontrunner Michael Watley. Polls currently show Cooper holding a firm 7-8% lead. In a state where elections are frequently won or lost by fractions of a percentage point, Cooper's lead is probably the second most impressive poll performance we've seen in recent state history. I say second most because it was only last year that North Carolina saw Democrat Josh Stein beat Republican Mark Robinson by almost 15 points. Stein won more than 3 million votes, the highest ever for a statewide candidate in North Carolina. And he was also the only Democrat to win a governorship in a state carried by Trump in 2024. Now, the 2026 Senate election is well over a year away, and anything can happen between now and election day. But these numbers show us that the race is already in full swing, both sides know the stakes, and they are playing for keeps.
