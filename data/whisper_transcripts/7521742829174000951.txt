Last hour, the FBI seized the phone of the CEO of PolyMarker after the betting platform predicted the victory of Donald Trump. The FBI agents raided the apartment in Manhattan of the CEO of PolyMarker, Shane Copland, on Wednesday morning, just a week after the electoral betting platform predicted Donald Trump's impressive victory, according to the New York Post. The 26-year-old businessman was awakened from his bed at 6 a.m. by the American police, who demanded that he hand over his phone and other electronic devices, a source close to the magnate said. It is the great political theater in its worst moment, the source said. They could have asked their lawyer for any of these things. Instead, they organized a raid to be able to leak it to the media.
