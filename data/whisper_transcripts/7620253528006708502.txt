Olivier Galsy is the new mayor of Avignon. The candidate for the winter election won the election against candidate PSLFI David Fournier and the candidate for the National Assembly Anne-Sophie Rigaud. So who is the new mayor of Avignon? Olivier Galsy is a face known by many French people. Journalist until 2020, he has presented many TV shows, including Télématin on France 2 or the morning news on ITV, the old name of CNews. Born in 1971 in Tunisia, Olivier Galsy studied in Avignon and then obtained a degree in political studies in Grenoble in 1994. He began his career as a journalist in 1993 and returned to France in 1995. He will remain in the French audiovisual landscape until 2020. At the end of 2025, to the great surprise, he is a candidate for the municipal elections of Avignon. He receives the support of the horizon party of Edouard Philippe and the UDI, two parties of the center-right. In his program, Olivier Galsy plans in particular the creation of 60 more municipal police stations, the implementation of video surveillance combined with artificial intelligence, but also the movement of the road station to streamline traffic. It remains to be seen whether the 6 years of his mandate will be enough to implement these great projects.
