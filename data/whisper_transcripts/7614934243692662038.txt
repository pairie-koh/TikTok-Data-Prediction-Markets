Can a market really predict elections, inflation and the outcome of geopolitical events in real time? Hi everyone, my name is Saad, I work at The Smart Money Show as the co-head of content where we break down financial ideas as well as career insights for students interested in finance. Now this question really sits at the heart of something new called prediction markets, where people can trade contracts linked to direct real world events and as traders react to new information, prices constantly adjust to reflect the market's new collective expectation. In a world shaped by geopolitical events, political uncertainty, these markets offer a brand new way to observe how expectations are forming in real time.
