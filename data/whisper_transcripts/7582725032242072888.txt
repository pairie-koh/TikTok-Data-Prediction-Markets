Did you know that there are people betting with cryptocurrencies on whether Maduro falls out of power or how the war in Ukraine advances? Yes, it sounds crazy, but it's happening. Thousands of users around the world are using platforms like PolyMarket and Kalshi to bet with cryptocurrencies on real events, from elections, wars, to who will be the character of the year of Time magazine. For example, in PolyMarket there are markets where people buy shares that are worth between 1 and 99 cents, depending on the probability that something happens. If the event happens, they earn $1 per share. And the most controversial thing, some users are betting with maps in real time on the conflict between Russia and Ukraine, seeing the advance of the troops live. Even the Institute for the Study of War, which creates one of those maps, publicly rejected its use for bets. There are also predictions about a possible military intervention in Venezuela or election winners in other countries. Experts warn about ethical risks because in the end they are monetizing political decisions and human tragedies. But what do you think?
