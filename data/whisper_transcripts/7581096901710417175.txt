One year ago, the FBI raided his apartment. Today, he's the world's youngest self-made billionaire at 27. Meet Shane Coplin. Born in 1998 in NYC, he was obsessed with markets early. At 14, he emailed the SEC asking how to build a marketplace. At 16, he walked into Genius HQ, one invited, and got hired. Dropped out of NYU after studying computer science. Bought Ethereum at ICO prices in 2014. In 2020, at just 21, he founded Polymarket, a prediction market built on smart contracts. And on Polymarket, you can bet on literally anything. Who wins the election? Will the Super Bowl go to overtime? Will Taylor Swift get married? During the 2024 election, users wagered $3.6 billion, and Polymarket correctly predicted the winner when pollsters couldn't. Overall, it has processed over $3.2 billion in trading volume. Then came the hit FBI raid November 2024. Devices seized. By mid-2025, all investigations dropped. Right after that, the biggest investors in tech lined up. Intercontinental Exchange invested $2 billion for up to 25% of the company, joined by Travis Kalanick, Dylan Field, Mark Pincus, Glenn Dubin valuation, $9 billion. Copeland Stake, 11%. Net worth, $1 billion. And the wildest part? Polymarket hasn't even launched in the US, and it's never turned a profit.
