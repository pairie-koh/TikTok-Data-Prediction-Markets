The Michigan Public Service Commission has approved another electric rate increase for DTE Energy, this time adding about 242 million dollars in annual revenue to your bill. This latest increase is the fourth major rate hike in five years, coming after a 217 million dollar increase in 2025 and others before that. Officials with the Michigan Public Service Commission say the extra revenue will go towards reliability improvements like tree trimming and grid updates and point out that electric service restoration times have dramatically improved. They also note that the approved hike was much smaller than what DTE originally requested. Critics including Michigan's Attorney General and advocacy groups say customers are tired of constant increases and worry bills are rising faster than wages, with Michigan already having some of the highest energy costs in the Midwest. DTE has reported strong financial results and profit growth in recent years while also paying executive compensation and dividends. Some residents argue that shareholders benefit even as customers pay more. As always, I'll keep you updated.
