Breaking news, Greece has intercepted ballistic missiles, but not over Greece. Greek Patriot system stationed in Saudi Arabia shot down two ballistic missiles this week right in the middle of the escalating Middle East conflict. The government says it's purely defensive, protecting infrastructure, protecting allies, no escalation. But inside Greece, there's an uproar. Opposition parties are reacting strongly, accusing the government of dragging the country deep into a war without clear public mandate. The critics are asked, when Greek-operated systems are actively intercepting missiles in a conflict zone, can you still call it non-involvement? This isn't new either. Greek missile systems have been in Saudi Arabia since 2021, originally to guard energy facilities. But now, with real interceptions happening, the role feels different. Less defensive presence, more active participation. So the question is, is Greece just defending allies, or already part of the conflict?
