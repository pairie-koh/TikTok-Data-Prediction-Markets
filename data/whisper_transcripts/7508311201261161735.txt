We might not have an Air 1 in Oz, but we do have a Harris Farm and I'm going to show you why I'm obsessed with this place and also some of my favorite things to buy from here. They actually just opened a brand new one in Merrickville and I spent a solid hour in here doing laps. You know you're getting old when grocery shopping becomes fun, but all the fresh produce in here was beautiful, like so colorful, so vibrant. They had this really cool nut butter station where you can fill up your own nut butters. Some of my favorite things to buy from here are obviously the fruit and veg, but I also love these tortilla chips and the hot salsa pairing, which I actually found out about from a Sophia in Sydney video a couple of years ago and I can attest it's amazing. Their fish is amazing. Their sashimi is beautiful. I grabbed some loco love. I grabbed some salmon prosciutto, a bunch of different dips. They have a pour your own honey station, a milk on tap station. These Bombay packs are unreal. Another huge fave of mine. Chocolate cocoa bella obviously is a must. The tiramisu here is also amazing. They've got pasta, we've got milk, we've got Sonoma bakery goods and a whole bunch of fresh fruit and veg again. I went back to stock up on some more because my cart needed some more color and life and health to it. Tony's Chocolony, really, really good. The milk chocolate sea salt caramel one. I did some serious damage, but feeling really positive about it and highly recommend Harris Farm. It is truly an adult playground. Have the best time.
