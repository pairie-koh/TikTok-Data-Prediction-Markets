The way the voting system is structured we see quite a few flaws. The online voter registration system, it seems to be an honor system. Anybody can put information in there to register to vote. All you have to do is click a box and say that you're not lying and then you'll get an email from the Secretary of State or something in the mail saying thank you for registering to vote and there you are. Once you're on the voter rolls anytime an election comes around guess what you get a mail-in ballot.
