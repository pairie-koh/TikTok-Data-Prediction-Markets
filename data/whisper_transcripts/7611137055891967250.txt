The measurement carried out by Invamer for Noticias Caracol and Blu Radio places Iván Cepeda in first place with 37.1%, in a scenario with 17 candidates. In second place, Abelardo de la Espriella appears with 18.9%, followed by Claudia López with 11.7%, Paloma Valencia with 10% and Sergio Fajardo with 6.6%. In that same scenario, the white vote reaches 2.4%. When the survey reduces the picture to 6 candidates in the first round, Cepeda rises to 43% and de la Espriella reaches 23.4%. Claudia López gets 12.5%, Sergio Fajardo gets 10.3% and Paloma Valencia gets 7.8%. The study also projects a possible second round between Iván Cepeda and Abelardo de la Espriella. In that scenario, 59.4% would vote for Cepeda, against 37.4% that would support de la Espriella. The white vote would reach 3.2%. Another key fact. 52.6% of the surveyed say they would vote for a candidate in favor of Gustavo Petro's government, while 43.5% would vote for an opponent. With these numbers, the electoral panorama enters a decisive phase, with a marked advantage at the top of the table and an electorate that begins to define itself in the face of the first presidential round.
