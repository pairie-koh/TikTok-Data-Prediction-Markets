Prediction markets are like the vape of finance. This has always entertained me because financial markets are prediction markets, but with one major distinction. Financial markets have order and stability because it's made up of both hedgers and speculators. Prediction markets are just the speculators. That isn't a market, that's a casino. Last time I checked, betting on what word was going to be in the next South Park episode is not a legitimate hedge. For the most part, prediction markets offer a new, easy, and more meaningless way to lose money than financial markets. And that is saying a lot.
