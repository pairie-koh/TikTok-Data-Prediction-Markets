Calgary police officers say inadequate staffing numbers are leading to safety risks, poor response times and overall burnout. That comes from an internal survey conducted this past summer. Only 11 per cent of those surveyed believe the service is adequately staffed, down from 14 per cent last year. Support for leadership is improving, up to 36 per cent from 30 last year. Mark Neufeld resigned as chief in May, with Katie McClellan taking over. Overall, employee morale has trended up for a third straight year. The chief says the service is understaffed. We continuously have to move our resources around when we have busy days, seven days a week. And so, as you alluded to, we're working hard with the commission to come forward with a budget. There will be a lot of asks for additional sworn members, because the short answer to your question is we are understaffed on the sworn side. More than 1,600 staff were polled online with a 49 per cent response rate.
