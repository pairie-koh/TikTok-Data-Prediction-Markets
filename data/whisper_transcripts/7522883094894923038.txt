Hi, my name is Rebecca Amidon, and I'm gearing up to launch my campaign for the Michigan State Senate in District 32. This is historically a very red district. However, the last Democrat to run for this seat lost by only 5.6 percent, and Lansing is calling this one of the most watched races in the state because of how flippable this seat is. When elected, one of my top priorities will be addressing the child care crisis, and you might be thinking, I don't have small children. Why should I care? According to the Chamber of Commerce, child care related issues cause a $2.8 billion loss to Michigan's economy annually. In addition, Michigan loses $576 million annually in tax revenue due to child care issues. These lost tax dollars could go towards public services and programs such as education, health care, transportation, and basic infrastructure. 14% of parents left the job in the past six months due to child care issues. This means that Michigan employers have a smaller pool of qualified candidates for hire. Addressing the child care crisis is an overwhelmingly popular opinion shared by both Republicans and Democrats. When surveyed, 69% of Republicans agreed with the 86% of Democrats that we need affordable child care. We have to start voting people into office who are willing to work across the aisle and create policies that benefit all Michiganders, regardless of party affiliation. This is why I'm running for the Michigan State Senate in District 32. While we gear up to launch this campaign, check out my website at RebeccaAmidon.com. Every contribution gets us one step closer to building a future that works for everyone in the 32nd District.
