Hot, the FBI is investigating PolyMarket, a market-based platform after the US presidential election because it is suspected of political manipulation. On November 7, when the US election market closed, PolyMarket announced that Donald Trump was the winner, with a total transaction volume of $ 3.6 billion. Interestingly, although the major polls showed a 50-50 support rate between Trump and Harris, PolyMarket showed a different result. Not long after, the FBI confiscated Shane Copland's phone and electronic devices, the founder of PolyMarket, at his home in New York. This move made many people doubt the true purpose of the investigation. According to the New York Post, the FBI is conducting this investigation under the pretext of manipulating the market and intending to make a favorable election result for Trump. Which this article describes as a political race from the current administration. Mr. Copland has spoken on social media, accusing the administration of falsely accusing their companies of not supporting him. He affirmed that PolyMarket always maintains a neutral stance and is proud of the value it brings to millions of users.
