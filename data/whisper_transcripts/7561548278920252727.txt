The midterm elections are closer than you think. So what are the closest races that'll decide the balance of power in the House? Then we have Don Davies from North Carolina's 1st district, who only won by 6,000 votes in 2024. And he currently has a 67% chance of keeping the seat blue. Next up, we have Vincente Gonzalez from Texas's 34th congressional district, who won by just 5,000 votes. The market's only giving him a 41% chance after Texas's tough redistricting. And finally, in one of the closest elections last election, that was only decided by 2,500 votes, we have Jared Golden from Maine's 2nd congressional district, who currently has just a 55% chance of winning re-election.
