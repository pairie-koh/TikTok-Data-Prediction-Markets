This is the news that Jacinta Allen did not want. We can reveal in Victoria Inc this week that popular local publican Andrew Lethleen is going to challenge her in Bendigo East. Lethleen attracted a swing of more than 9% at last year's federal election against Labor MP Lisa Chesters. Now, he's going to face an uphill battle. Allen holds the seat on a margin of more than 10%. But there's evidence she has been concerned. Recently, she's been posting on social media about door-knocking her local electorate, something she hasn't done for quite a while. In any event, the prospect of the Premier defending her own seat is not good for Labor. This will divert resources and Allen's time away from the Melbourne seats she needs to defend against the Liberals. The other question is this, will the Libs give the Nats a free run or will they field a candidate of their own? Regardless, Jacinta Allen is going to be spending a lot more time in her backyard than she bargained for.
