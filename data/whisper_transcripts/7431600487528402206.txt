Election betting has been banned for a hundred years, but this company is bringing it back. Election betting used to be super popular in America, accurately predicting 14 out of 15 presidential races between 1884 and 1940. Cal State took the CFTC to court to grant approval for election trading, and they won. With Cal State, you can now invest in your predictions on political events. That matter? Literally any event.
