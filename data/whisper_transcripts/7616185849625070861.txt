A federal judge in Ohio has ruled that prediction markets are evading the law, and Pierre's Bobby Allen reports there are more than 20 federal lawsuits pending over the future of the betting craze. U.S. District Judge Sarah Morrison says apps like Kalshi and Polly Market are no different than gambling. Kalshi argued it should not be regulated like a gambling operation because it's technically a type of futures contract, not a gambling site. But billions of dollars are spent every week on Kalshi where people bet on who will win the latest season of Survivor, whether Trump will say Midnight Hammer, and how many times sports announcers will say the word foul. In her ruling, Morrison wrote, Treating this as anything other than gambling is absurd. Kalshi says it plans to appeal.
