Arizona just filed criminal charges against Kelshi, the first time any U.S. state has criminally charged a major prediction market. Attorney General Chris Mays says Kelshi is running an illegal gambling operation disguised as a futures exchange and it never got state approval to operate. Prediction markets have exploded. Traders now spend about $5 billion every week on sites like Kelshi and PolyMarket. The industry already faces scrutiny over Iran war death markets and insider trading concerns. Now, Kelshi's executives face a criminal case in Maricopa County Superior Court and every other prediction market in the country is watching to see if their state is next.
