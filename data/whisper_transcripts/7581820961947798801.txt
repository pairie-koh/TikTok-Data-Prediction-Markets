PolyMarket is being accused of double-counting its trading volume, by almost 100%. According to new research amplified by Paradigm's Matt Huang. PolyMarket's smart contracts emit two order-filled events for every trade, one for the maker, one for the taker. Most third-party dashboards simply add both. Which means they've been reporting almost double the real volume. The real numbers? Around $1.25 billion per month, not the $2.5 billion you might have seen. PolyMarket says its own site uses proper metrics. But data providers like Defilama and BlockWorks are now updating their dashboards after reviewing the findings. What do you think? Honest data complexity or competitive drama?
