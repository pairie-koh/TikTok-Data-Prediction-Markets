The FBI finds the home of Pauli Market founder Shane Copeland in connection to the bets made prior to the presidential election. This morning FBI agents broke into Copeland's apartment, founder and CEO of the company. According to the New York Times and Bloomberg, the search in New York is related to a criminal investigation led by the FBI and the district attorney's office in southern New York. It is suspected that Pauli Market could have operated as a commodities market without a license, allowing users in the United States to bet illegally despite a previous agreement with the federal government. Agents seized Copeland's electronic devices, including his phone, in search of evidence. Pauli Market gained relevance for its probabilities on the presidential election, where it greatly favored Donald Trump over Kamala Harris.
