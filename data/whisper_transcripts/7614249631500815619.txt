Betting that Ali Khamenei dies? This is the platform PolyMarket. In its own words, the largest market for predictions of the world. With crypto, you can bet on all sorts of things here. For example, who will win the football world championship in the USA, Canada and Mexico in 2026. But also, when will Ali Khamenei no longer be the spiritual leader of Iran? And when will the USA actually attack Iran? According to media reports, a user named MagaMyMan bet around 87,000 US dollars on an official end of the Ayatollah at the end of February, just a few days before the Israeli air strike in which Khamenei was killed. In total, MagaMyMan has won more than half a million dollars with several predictions. And he wasn't the only one. According to research, six other users bet on this exact date just a few hours before the US and Israeli attack on Iran on February 28th. And together they just won 1.2 million dollars. And now it's getting really crazy. Some of the suspicious accounts are said to have only been reopened hours before the attack. That this is legal is insane, says the Democratic US Senator Chris Murphy on Axe. He wanted to introduce a bill as soon as possible to ban bets on war and death. Murphy even suggested that people from the environment of US President Trump take advantage of it. But he didn't provide evidence for his allegations.
