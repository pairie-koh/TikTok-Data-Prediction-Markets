Most people have no idea how prediction markets like polymarkets actually price risk, so I'm building a bot to trade them. This is a new series where I'm developing a quantitative system for polymarket bitcoin prediction markets using the clob order book to detect when implied probability is mispriced and then execute systematically. This isn't technical analysis. In a clob, the edge lives in microstructure spreads, depth, queue position, fee drag, and short horizon noise. A marker can look efficient on the surface, but once you model frictions properly, mispricings show up. Or you find out there's no edge and you have to move on. Over the next episodes, I'm showing the whole process, build in public, data ingestion from the order book, converting prices into applied probabilities, filtering noise, stress testing across 15 minute, one hour, and four hour horizons, and then automating execution. Day one is pretty simple. We're going to define the exact market, the data fields we need, and the first pass of the pricing plus execution rules. If you want to see a real quant build from scratch, you can follow along this series.
