I thank you from the bottom of my heart. As for the rest, Waheguru has been very kind. I request the entire Sangat that we will not celebrate any celebrations until the 6th Reek. The sacrifice of our martyrs, the victory of our victory is also dedicated to the martyrs. I request the entire Sangat that we will take rest for a while. Tomorrow, I and Gurdwara Saheb will wear the Pogh for the 6th Reek. After that, we will do whatever program is necessary.
