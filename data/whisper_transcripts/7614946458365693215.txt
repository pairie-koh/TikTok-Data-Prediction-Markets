Only 10 to 15 percent of registered voters show up for local and state elections. That means about 90 percent of registered voters are not represented in local government because these voters are not participating in the vote. Local elections includes mayor, city council, county commissioner, state representative, and state senators. You do have congress at the federal level, but also at the state level. A lot of people don't even know what districts they're in. Most voters show up for the presidential election and that's still only about 50 to 60 percent of registered voters. It does take extra time to research who you're voting for, which district or ward you are in, but it's definitely worth it. I know many people who would make great candidates or public officials and they don't run for office because they know that people won't show up to vote. So when more people are showing up to vote and actually care about who their representatives are, that actually helps inspire and encourage really good candidates and people who know that they would be supported if they did run for office. And we definitely want to encourage good people to run for office.
