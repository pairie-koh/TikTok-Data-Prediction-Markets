This is the last market at Victoria Park hosted by Open Circle, happening today 22nd of March till 9pm. We saw this caramel apple stand for the first time, so we got the half pretzel half choc cookie flavour to try. I actually enjoyed them way more than I thought I would. The caramel sauce on the pretzel one is so good with the sour green apple. I have been craving burnt out pizza, I just can't get over how airy their focaccia base is. This horchata tiramisu is pretty good, but we loved the pistachio one even more. We loved Grillshark's ribs from the other market, so we tried their new sliders this time. The patties are sweet, savoury and smoky. First time trying JJ Steiner and the Seattle hot dog was perfect on a hot day. And we grabbed matcha and some popcorn to go. Okay bye!
