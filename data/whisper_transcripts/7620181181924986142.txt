A Khalistan referendum is being held in the US city of Seattle, with thousands of Sikhs expected to participate as part of the global campaign for an independent Sikh homeland. The vote is being organized by Sikhs for Justice under the leadership of Guru Patwan Singh Pannu. Organizers said Khalistan flags have been displayed across downtown Seattle, including a large flag installed in the central location. Members of the Sikh community have traveled from various regions to take part in the referendum, which is first of its kind in Seattle. Similar referendums have previously held in the cities such as London, Toronto, Vancouver, Rome and Geneva, while a similar vote was conducted last year in Ottawa. The Indian government has consistently opposed such referendums, although the US authorities have permitted the event to take place. Sikh leaders allege that the members of their community are being targeted abroad. The issue gained wider attention following the killings of Sikh leadership Hardeep Singh Nijjar in Canada in June 2023. Speaking on the matter, Pannu said that if the India considers the referendum insignificant, it should explain actions alleged taken against the Sikh leaders overseas, adding that the movement would continue without fear.
