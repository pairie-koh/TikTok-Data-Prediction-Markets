Our local deli shop make the best sandwiches. There's no menu here, but for $15, you can get whatever you want in a focaccia sandwich, which they make fresh every day. We really love their prosciutto, and they absolutely load it up. And you can pick from so many fillings. Their house-made chili oil is a must too. I mean, have you ever seen a more beautiful sandwich? Wait until I show you the texture of the prosciutto. It is so, so beautiful, so tasty. My husband and I have been coming here twice a week now. This is Raineri's in Five Dock.
