Hey guys, it's Yesenia. Come with me to Once Upon a Slush located in downtown Petaluma. Tucked away in a small courtyard in the alleyway near Putnam Plaza. They're known for their creative slushies, their soft serve ice cream, and their unique combo treats. What makes Once Upon a Slush so special is their slush plus soft serve layered combo. Which my little sister and I, we got the strawberry slush with vanilla soft serve and the cherry slush with vanilla soft serve. And they were both 10 out of 10. They have tons of changing flavors including some unique ones like pickled slush. They also serve simple foods like grilled cheese, hot dogs, popcorn, and even soup. While the menu draws you in, the real gem of Once Upon a Slush is the story. It all goes back to 2008 when the owners founded Westside Stories Petaluma. A production company built around storytelling. But to really understand how Once Upon a Slush began, let's hear it from the owner himself. My daughter was going to San Francisco State and she was driving all the way up to Healdsburg to get this ice cream. And we said, you can't find ice cream in San Francisco? She said, not like this. So we went with her one time up to Healdsburg and we talked to the owner and we decided we would start a franchise. We started here a little over seven years ago. And when our contract was up with the franchise, we left the franchise because we wanted to do some extra things. Make things drinkable, put some food on the menu. And she didn't want to do that, so we left the franchise. We became Once Upon a Slush. We are Once Upon a Slush because we also run a storytelling show here in town called Westside Stories. So we kept with the theme and there we are and here we are today. Thank you. My pleasure. I love getting to learn the story behind Once Upon a Slush. And if you haven't had the chance to go buy yet, I definitely recommend trying their different slush and soft serve combinations.
