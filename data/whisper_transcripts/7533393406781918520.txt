PolyMarket is the largest decentralized prediction market platform in the world. It was founded in 2020 and allows users to interact with results of political, sporting, economic, cultural, and usually hits up to 93% of the results. Verifying participations that represent options between yes and no, whose price fluctuates between $ 0 and $ 1, reflects the estimated probability that an event will occur. This platform has shown great effectiveness in predicting events, for example, in 2024 in the presidential elections of the United States. PolyMarket anticipated more accurately the victory of Donald Trump, some users generated millions thanks to their prediction by interpreting the public sentiment. These predictions based on real incentives are usually much more agile, and in many cases more accurate than traditional methods.
