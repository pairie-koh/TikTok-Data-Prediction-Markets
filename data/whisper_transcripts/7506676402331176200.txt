Former Commander-in-Chief Ruen Aguanzon questioned the Commission on Elections after the ballots with overvotes reached almost 30% after the midterm elections in 2025. According to NAMFREL, more than 17 million overvotes were registered in the ballots for the Senate, which is higher than the previous elections. According to statistics from 2010 to 2022, only 1.5 to 2.5% of the ballots had overvotes. But this year, 29.89% or almost 30% overvoted. In his social media post, Aguanzon explained that this number is absurd or not credible and that there is a possibility that the voters will doubt the legitimacy of the ballots. In front of the deepening debate, Aguanzon called on the Comelec to explain to the public so that the integrity of the results of the elections will not be doubted.
