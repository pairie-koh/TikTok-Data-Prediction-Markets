A man accused of randomly shooting a pregnant woman inside her car has been found not guilty by reason of insanity. That shooting happened in the Belltown neighborhood in 2023. 34 year old Ina Kwan and her unborn daughter were both killed. Her husband was seriously injured. Cordell Goosby was charged with first degree murder. But by presenting a not guilty by reason of insanity defense, Goosby agrees that he committed the crimes but had a mental disease at the time per Washington state law. Defense first obtained their own expert report and their expert found the defendant was insane at the time of this crime. The state then obtained our own expert who also evaluated the defendant and they came to that same conclusion that he was insane at the time of the crime. Goosby will now go to a state psychiatric hospital where he could spend the rest of his life.
