There's a 750-mile fault line under the U.S. west coast that's been building pressure for over a century. When part of it finally blows, it'll make the 1906 San Francisco earthquake look like nothing. Scientists say when one of the major west coast faults snap, it could unleash a magnitude 7 to 9 quake shaking for up to three minutes. And the worst part?
