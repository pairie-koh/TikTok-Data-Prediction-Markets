It took me 22 years to understand what this Harry Potter scene actually meant. Wait for it. Tell me, what would I get if I added powdered root of asphodel to an infusion of wormwood? So Snape mentions asphodel and wormwood. Well, check this out. Asphodel is a flower member of the lily family. And according to Victorian flower language, it means my regrets follow you to the grave. While wormwood symbolizes absence. So he was sending a coded message to Harry, saying how much he regrets Lily's death.
