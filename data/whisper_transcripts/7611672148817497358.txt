Breaking news, we are seeing an influx of dark money flow into the Democratic primary in North Carolina's 4th Congressional District. Yes, as Nida Allam, who took our political integrity pledge, became the leader in this race, an AI super PAC, as well as a super PAC with ties to AIPAC, has dumped over $2.2 million into this race a week before election day to support incumbent Valerie Foushee. The AI super PAC, Jobs and Democracy PAC, funded by Anthropic, has already spent $1.6 million and Article One PAC, a dark money super PAC with ties to AIPAC, has sprung up overnight and already spent over $600,000. Nida Allam has her own super PAC support with American Priority PAC, a PAC that's designed to be a counterweight to AIPAC, already spending $900,000 in her race. Their funding is from a few public but large individual donors, and this is the first election they've been involved in. The PACs of Leaders We Deserve, Justice Democrats, and the Working Families Party have all spent between $100,000 and $200,000 in support of Nida Allam, although it is worth noting that the majority of the funding for those PACs does come from small individual donations under $200.
