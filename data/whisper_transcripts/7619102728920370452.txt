I went to the harris taper sample sale to see if it's actually worth your time. I got there about 30 minutes early and it was already open which we love. There were size 6-14 pieces but mostly size 8's and everything was organised by size which makes life so much easier shopping. I will say there were quite a few faulty items which I personally love because I just fix them up and get a good deal but a lot of them were still sitting around the $200 mark which I found a bit high. There was a really good mix though, knits, jackets, pants, skirts, tops, just a bit of everything. I also spotted a faulty Eliza top for $200 so if you go that's a little gem waiting for someone. If you're a little closer to $10 there was definitely a line forming so I'd recommend going early if you can. They've also got a big communal changing room with a few mirrors which is great for trying things on. Compared to last year I'd say their pricing is a bit higher but if you're in Auckland it's on today and tomorrow.
