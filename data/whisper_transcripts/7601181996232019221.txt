PolyMarket was officially banned in Vietnam after a warning from the Cyber Security Bureau. According to the warning, this platform is suspected to be related to piracy, violating the current law. The authorities emphasize that access and participation may be restricted to serve processing work. Previously, PolyMarket was also restricted in more than 30 countries around the world. This trend shows that Vietnam is tightening control over platforms that are not allowed and users need to be especially careful during this sensitive period.
