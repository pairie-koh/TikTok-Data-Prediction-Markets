National cheesesteak day is coming up on March 24th and in 2026 I'm celebrating with Penn Station. Here I am with their famous cheesesteak which you can get for $4.99 only on March 24th. This cheesesteak which honestly was delicious paired with their house cut fries and fresh squeezed lemonade and we couldn't go and see a sign for new cheese dipper sauce and not get it because what does a cheesesteak need more than more cheese you can dip it into. I went with my husband and we also got an Italian toasted and did halfsies and ended on a sweet note with those cute but not so cute you can't devour the chocolate chip cookies. Out of all the fast casual spots to choose from I liked Penn Station wasn't hiding anything. All their ingredients were on display and they were making all sandos to order. Now the question is will I wait until national cheesesteak day on March 24th to return or will I be returning much sooner?
