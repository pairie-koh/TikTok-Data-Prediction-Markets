The popular online prediction market company Polly Market is opening a pop up bar in DC tonight. Online prediction markets allow users to wager on the outcomes of real world events such as elections and current affairs. Here's a look at the bar. Polly Market is calling it the situation room and says it will be the world's first bar dedicated to monitoring current events. The company says the space will feature live X feeds, flight radar, stock market data and other news sources. 60th Street Northwest near 21st Street.
