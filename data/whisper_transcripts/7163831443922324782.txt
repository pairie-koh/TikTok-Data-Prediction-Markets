There are dozens of races tonight, but here are three to keep an eye on. First, the governor's race in New York, where the incumbent, Kathy Hoko, a Democrat, has been caught off guard by the insurgent campaign of Lee Zeldin. If he wins, it tells me that Democrats didn't take concerns of public safety seriously enough. The second race I'm watching, J.D. Vance and Tim Ryan for a Senate seat from Ohio. Ryan is a mainstream Democrat. Vance, on the other hand, used to be a Trump critic who is now an ardent Trump supporter, making it seem like he will take any position to get elected. If he does, it means that the Trump brand remains strong. Third race I'm watching is for a House representative seat in New Jersey. Tom Malinowski, a Democrat, is fighting off Tom Kane, a Republican. Malinowski is a centrist Democrat, hasn't taken a lot of controversial positions. If he loses tonight, what that tells me is that suburban voters are looking to take out their frustration and to punish Democrats for what they see as the slow pace of returning to the pre-pandemic normal.
