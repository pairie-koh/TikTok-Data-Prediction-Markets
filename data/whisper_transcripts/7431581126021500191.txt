People really mistake what a probability is. I see it all the time on Twitter. Trump is leading by 20 points, 60, 40. That's not a 20 point lead when it's talking about probability. So being it's football season, I've tried to put this in the football metaphor and I'll use Trump. If he is trading under 66%, which he is, and has been since Harris came into the race, think about it as in a football game, he's leading by less than three points. All she's gotta do is get the ball back, kick a field goal, and she wins the game. If he leads by more than 66 to 80%, then he's leading in football parlance between three and seven points. She's gotta get the ball back and score a touchdown. It's a little harder. If he's leading by more than 80%, he's leading by more than seven points in a football metaphor. Now she's gotta score twice.
