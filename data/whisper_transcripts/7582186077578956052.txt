What exactly is the PonyMarket set-up? Why does a prediction platform have a set-up opportunity? This is a frequently asked question by my DApp clients recently. Today, I will make it clear in two minutes. First of all, you need to know that PonyMarket's bidding is not a centralized bid to buy big or small, but to buy two tokens, yes and no. In theory, the price of yes and no must be equal to $1. For example, yes is 0.6, and no is normally 0.4. At the end of the event, the wrong token will be cleared, and the right token will be worth $1. There is no problem with this logic. But the key is that the price of yes and no of PonyMarket is not set by the platform, but the result of global users' mutual transactions. As long as someone is emotional, misjudged, and impulsive, the price will be very low. So you will often see that yes is 0.6, no is 0.35, and the total price is 0.95. At this time, there is a 0.05 risk-free space. Professional teams will buy both yes and no tokens at the same time. The total cost is 0.95. No matter what the final result is, the settlement will become $1, and the profit of 0.05 is $100,000, which is a profit of $5,000. The second reason is that the global information response is different. For example, if the U.S. suddenly announces data, the U.S. user will react in 10 seconds. Asian users are still sleeping. At this time, the price will be misplaced instantly. Yes will be bought at 0.7 instantly, and no will fall to 0.33. The total price is $1.03. The settlement team will sell two tokens, yes and no, and get a 3% profit. The third reason is that many events in the Poly Market are very low-flow. A large order of tens of thousands of dollars will blow the price to the pool. Yes may hit 0.5, no at 0.45, and the total price is 0.95, and there will be a 5% chance of settlement. But ordinary people can't keep an eye on so many markets. A professional settlement team will use robots to monitor dozens of events for 24 hours. As long as the price is misplaced, it will be executed instantly. The fourth reason is that the prediction market itself is a probability market. Probability will be strongly influenced by emotions. For example, in an election, as long as a fan of a candidate buys Yes madly, Yes will be pushed to 0.65, and no will fall to 0.3. The total price is 0.95. There will be another settlement window. The last source is even more hidden. Cross-platform settlement. For example, Poly Market and Cash will predict an event at the same time. The Yes of Poly Market is 0.8, and the No is 0.2. The Yes of Cash is 0.75, and the No is 0.25. Settlers only need to buy the No token on Poly Market, and then buy the Yes token on Cash. Both sides add up to 0.95, which can cover all the results of the event. In theory, you can get 5% profit. To sum up, Poly Market's settlement is not a platform to send money, but a price error created by the market participants who do not understand the probability, depth, and price.
