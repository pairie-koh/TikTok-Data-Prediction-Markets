My name is Alyssa Chen and I'm a data reporter with the Minnesota Reformer. Prediction markets like Polly Market and Kalshi allow people to bet on almost anything, including sports, politics, and even war. They've been skirting state regulations on gambling, including in Minnesota, where sports betting is currently illegal. They're doing this by claiming that they're selling events futures contracts instead of bets, although they explicitly advertise as gambling platforms. State regulators around the country are now trying to close this loophole, including in Minnesota, where lawmakers this week introduced a bill to ban prediction markets.
