Am I the only one confused about Polymarket? Why is everyone betting on everything? There's been bets on mayoral elections, on who's going to be the new pope, on whether Israel would launch a new ground offensive in Gaza, even on whether the Haktuwo girl was going to have Trump appear on her podcast as a guest. Which he didn't, but there's still a 1% chance, so you never know. And we're not even talking about small bets. All this betting has made the Polymarket CEO the youngest self-made billionaire in history. But it's also turned the world into one giant auction, where people who are struggling to get by are hoping to get rich quick by online betting on everything.
