Under the Presidential Elections Act, Section 57 hereby declares the final results of the presidential election in exercise of the powers conferred upon the Electoral Commission by Article 103 by 7 of the Constitution. The Commission publishes the results as follows. Kiagulani Sentamu Robert and UP, Yoweri Kakuta Museveni, 43.2%. Kiagulani Sentamu Robert, having obtained the highest number of votes in the election and the votes cast in his favour being more than 50%, 50% of the valid votes cast at the election. I, Justice Simon Baibakama, Chairperson of the Electoral Commission, hereby declare Kiagulani Sentamu Robert elected President of the Republic of Uganda. Signed and sealed this 17th day of January 2026.
