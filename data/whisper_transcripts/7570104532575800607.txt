Will Trump visit North Korea in 2025? Right now, it's trading at 5 cents. That means, based on the people betting, the market thinks there's a 5% chance it happens. But it's not a poll. And if I'm wrong, I lose money. So people only really push the price when they're confident they know something everyone else doesn't. And that's what makes production markets so wild. The odds are a live scoreboard of what the internet actually believes, weighted by risk. Not just what people say, but what they're willing to bet on. Not only that, these markets are often more accurate than polls. Because if you're not confident enough to risk losing cash, your opinion doesn't move the market.
