And yet our video of Haus des Döners has reached more than 3 million views overnight. But how do we manage to produce viral videos continuously for over three years? These three factors are the basis for virality on social media. First, the algorithm loves relevance. If you pick up topics that are in the news or trends, your video automatically gets more reach. Second, visuals. Through the warning vest the boy wears, an indirect hint is given to the current topic, namely the robber's outfit. And what else is added are the micro hints such as vacation in Paris, chain or uncle's cream company. Third, that the cream company comes from Paris causes discussion in the comments. If your brand should finally go viral, start working on current topics in your content.
