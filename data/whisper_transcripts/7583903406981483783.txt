What if you can make money just by guessing the right outcome? Cause apparently you can, and it's called prediction markets. Now, prediction markets let people bet on real world events, sync, elections, sports, crypto, anything. Basically, instead of just having an opinion, you can actually take a position in what you believe will happen. Now, this idea isn't new, but it's actually going mainstream now. Each market has a yes or no contract and the price shows the probability. For example, if a yes contract trades at 70 cents, market thinks there's a 70% chance that that event happens. When the event is over, the winning side settles at $1 and the losing side, zero. Platforms like Polymarket and Kaoshi have absolutely exploded this year. Trading volumes hit record highs during big events, elections, CPI releases, Fed decisions, you name it. So basically prediction markets went from niche to mainstream dashboards of what people think will happen. Prediction markets have essentially become real time sentiment trackers used by traders, analysts, and even hedge funds. It's like a outsourced probability engine for anything that happens in the world. And honestly, this might be the next big frontier for how people forecast the future.
