The race between Kamala Harris and Donald Trump is literally closer than a coin flip. That's what a top pollster is saying in his final prediction for the US election results. Nate Silver says there has never been anything like this, with the odds being about as close to 50-50 as you can possibly get. Out of 80,000 simulations ran by his model, Kamala won 50.015% of the time, giving her an extremely thin lead to become the next president. But this could be the closest election in US history.
