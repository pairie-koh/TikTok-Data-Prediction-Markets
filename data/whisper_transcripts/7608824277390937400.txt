Ashton Hearn's team faces the possibility of being wiped off the map at risk of losing all 13 of its seats. Ashton Hearn's eyes are firmly on the Premier but perhaps it's the orange tsunami she should be watching. Polls they come and go. None of it takes away from my focus which is on getting out there and announcing policies that are genuinely going to improve the lives of South Australians. The numbers only marginally better in a YouGov survey. One Nation still outpaces the Libs while Labor stays firmly in control.
