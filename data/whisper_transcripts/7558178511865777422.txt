When you look at the quality of construction, this is a Harris pontoon. You can tell the high quality ones versus the low quality by how they do their end caps. If you look, you can see that the bracing comes all the way to the end. You've got a nice seven layer marine grade plywood. The flooring comes all the way to the end. And all they do is cap it to finish it off. And you can see how long these bolts are going all the way through. Not all manufacturers finish their boats off all the way to the edge with their construction. It's one way you can tell a premium level boat from a value in mid tier boat.
