Today, the Michigan Senate met for the first time since the tragic mass shooting at Michigan State University and Democrats in the Senate acted quickly to introduce legislation requiring universal background check, safe storage laws, and extreme risk protection order laws that can allow guns to be taken from people who judges deem are dangerous to themselves or others. This is the first step that we need to take here in Michigan to enact common sense gun safety laws and I'm really hopeful that now that for the first time in 40 years Democrats are in control of the Michigan legislature, we can move on to action on gun safety.
