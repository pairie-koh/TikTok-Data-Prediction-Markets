South Australians have wasted no time having their say in the state election with a record number casting their ballots in the first four days of early voting. As Michaela Kamarick reports both leaders are now scrambling to win over undecided voters as One Nation continues to gain traction. Well both major party leaders are throwing everything at South Aussies yet to make up their mind but many have already locked in their votes. More than 200,000 ballots have been cast since pre-polling opened on Saturday. With so many votes already banked the campaign appears to be shifting less about big new promises and more about selling what's already on the table. The Libs are continuing to push their 1.3 billion dollar cost of living package. Liberal leader Ashton Hearn promising to drive down costs for businesses. I think it's pretty clear to say that under Labor small businesses have got doughnuts whereas if a Hearn Liberal government is elected we'll be making life a little bit sweeter through some tax cuts. Now Peter Malinowski is leaning into his track records brooking Labor's ability to land major events. I'm pursuing every last opportunity for our state right around the place and I see major events as being a platform to drive economic growth and it's working. From today people can register their interest in tickets to the 2027 MotoGP which is coming to the streets of Adelaide. Now one of the most intriguing storylines of this campaign has been One Nation's surging support in the polls. The party is confident of a strong primary vote but Saturday will be the ultimate test whether their popularity will translate into seats in the lower house.
