If you think the reason Kakashi wears a mask is because he's embarrassed that his face resembles his father's, that reason is actually wrong. Why is it wrong? Because Kakashi has been wearing a mask since he was a child, even before his father passed away. So, what is the real reason Kakashi wears a mask? This is something that is not clearly explained in the anime, especially directly from Kakashi's character. But some theories suggest that Kakashi wears the mask because he wants to hide his emotions, as he has suffered from loss since he was young and even became a killer. However, in my opinion, this doesn't make sense because Kakashi had been wearing the mask since he was at the academy, a time when he hadn't lost anyone close to him or become a killer yet. According to Masashi Kishimoto, the reason Kakashi wears a mask is simply because he is a ninja who should conceal half of his face. Meanwhile, in the Naruto Chibi version, where the main character is Rock Lee, the reason Kakashi wears a mask is to cover his nosebleeds when reading Jiraiya's adult books.
