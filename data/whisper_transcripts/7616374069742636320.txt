It is not easy to be the elder of a family, because you already have responsibilities that you have not chosen. You will always have to be strong, even when you are broken inside, because it is you who must set an example for your brothers and sisters, when you have them. But remember this, God has entrusted you with this role, because he knows that you are able to assume it. So, do not give up, your success will come, and it will bless your whole family. Courage to you!
