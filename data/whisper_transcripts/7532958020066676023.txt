Hey everyone, it's Jay Chody from the North Carolina State Senate. Yesterday afternoon, the State Senate voted on legislation that opted the state of North Carolina into the federal voucher program that was part of Donald Trump's so-called Big Beautiful Bill. And as a product of Cumberland County Public Schools and a parent of children who attend Wake County Public Schools, I've got two real concerns about the bill. Number one, the bill establishes a dollar-for-dollar tax credit, a tax credit that moves money away from the federal government, moves money away from traditional public schools. And number two, the federal voucher program benefits the wealthiest families in our state, just like the Opportunity Scholarship Program at the state level, families, frankly, that needed the least. North Carolina is suffering from a public education crisis. Our teacher pay ranks 39th in the country. Our starting pay ranks 42nd. Our per-pupil spending is among the lowest in the country. This bill may make North Carolina the first to opt into a federal voucher program, but it certainly moves us closer to ranking last when it comes to teacher pay and per-student spending in public education.
