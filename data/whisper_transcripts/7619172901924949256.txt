The U.S. Mint could soon start producing 24-carat gold coins with Donald Trump's face on them. The U.S. Commission of Fine Arts, a panel handpicked by President Trump himself, approved the final design for the coin to celebrate America's 250th birthday on the 4th of July. U.S. federal law says no living president can appear on U.S. currency, but officials claim the Treasury Secretary can bypass that law.
