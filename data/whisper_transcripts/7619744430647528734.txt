A platform called Kalshi is operating a secret scheme of betting in elections and nobody is talking about that. What looks like just a prediction market is actually a system disguised as political bets, where people are putting money in electoral results as if it were a casino, but with real impact in the world. Experts warn that this can influence decisions, manipulate narratives and even create artificial pressure on the candidates. And the strangest thing is that all this would be happening under a supposed veil of legality, while the regulatory bodies avoid giving clear answers. Reports indicate suspicious movements, large numbers being bet on specific scenarios and anonymous users influencing trends as if they knew something before everyone else. In the networks, the debate has already become chaotic, some defend it as a financial innovation, others say that this is the beginning of a system where elections become a game of bets. And now comes the most intriguing part. There are those who believe that these platforms not only predict results, but also help manipulate them. The question is, are we witnessing the birth of a global political casino or is this already happening for much longer than we imagine?
