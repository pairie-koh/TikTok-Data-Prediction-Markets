The FBI just raided the home of a gambling website's CEO after the company correctly predicted the presidential election results. This is the gambling site, Polly Market. And they held an election prediction bet that put Trump's odds much higher than Kamala's, even though national polls predicted it to be a very close race. Polly Market brought in billions of dollars of bets on this election. And it started to become a question of who was making all these bets and was it influencing how people voted? Polly Market got sued a few years ago and settled the case and agreed to not allow US users to use their site. So Americans aren't allowed to use Polly Market. But it's apparently an open secret on social media that you can easily get around this if you're an American by using cryptocurrency. Big surprise. The shady part here is that Polly Market had been paying for targeted ads to American users who technically aren't allowed to use the site. They even paid US influencers to tweet about betting on the election. Almost half of the ads that Polly Market placed only had Trump's photo on them. Others had both candidates. None of them had just Kamala. Some with the tagline, don't trust the polls, trust the markets, which ended up being actually totally correct. The polls were way off in this election.
