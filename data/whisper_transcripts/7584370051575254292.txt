What is PolyMarket? How does it work? PolyMarket is the world's largest prediction market, supported by leading investors including IC, Market, the owner of New York Stock Exchange, MYSE, and Vitalik Buterin, and is a strategic partner of Google and Yahoo Finance. PolyMarket operates as a market where you can invest and predict events in the future through blockchain with a share price from the predicted reaction market. When the result is determined, the smart contract automatically pays for those who predict correctly, no need for intermediaries.
