You may or may not have seen the brand new Geo Dart released from Harrows just last week. Well, here we have all three barrel shapes. Parallel, Torpedo and Bomb. Three unique profiles, one seriously impressive dart. First up, the Parallel. A clean, straight profile built for consistency and control. The precision milled grip runs the full length, giving you that locked in feel with every throw. Next up, the Torpedo. Slightly tapered for a natural finger placement and smooth, confident release. If you like a barrel that guides your grip and adds that bit of finesse, this one's for you. And finally, my personal favourite, the Bomb. Chunkier, perfectly balanced and built for power and precision. Ideal if you love that solid feel in the hand and a stable flight to the board. And all three come with the brand new Quick Point technology, letting you switch your point length in just seconds. They're all paired with the Midi Solo Flight and Aero Cortec for that extra stability and less drag. And all finished in that slick blue titanium nitrate. What a gorgeous dart these are.
