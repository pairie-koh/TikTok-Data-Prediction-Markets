The PolyMarket prediction platform has been blocked from all access in Vietnam. Accordingly, the images circulated from yesterday, January 30, show that the PolyMarket, the world's number one prediction market platform, has now been blocked from access in Vietnam. Some images also show the emergence of the Cybersecurity and Crime Prevention using high-tech A0-5 Police Department in the access interface, making PolyMarket seen as an online monetization platform. And all access behaviors, participation in transactions or related activities can constitute violations of Article 321 and Article 322 of the Criminal Code. As of now, most IP addresses in Vietnam are no longer accessible on this platform, and Vietnam is no exception. When there are more than 30 countries in the world adopting restrictions or banning PolyMarket for similar reasons related to piracy and illegal activities, it is not clear. Although PolyMarket is built on blockchain and often identifies itself as a prediction market, in essence, it is still a form of money laundering, predicting the outcome of an event, then receiving a reward based on the correct result. This makes the platform fall directly into piracy, violating the current Vietnamese law. Recently, this platform also appeared in a post predicting a dispute over the candidate who will be elected to the position of Vietnam's Secretary-General with a maximum amount of more than 2 million USD, which is about 52 billion VND.
