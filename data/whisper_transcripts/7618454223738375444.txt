Good afternoon to you all. My name is Darzel Martinez, one of the representatives of the Filipino community of Manurewa High School. Some stories are told through words, ours is told through movements, rhythms, and the way our culture honors the land. We begin with Dosayan, a sacred chant of the Kalinga people of the Cordilleras, offered to Gabonanda creator Sky God. Its calm sound flows with our fan dance like quiet water. Next is Pangalay, meaning a gift offering. Its slow, seamless movements reflects the natural cycles of life. You will then see a fisherman returning home and a farmer planting into the soil, a simple reminder that our lives depend on the earth. Our Kabatingan farming dance follows the journey of rice from seed to harvest. Honoring their resilience and respect for the land. And as we end with Halit ng Lupa, the song of the earth, we leave you with this. If the environment lives in us, then every choice you make is a promise to protect it. Not just for today, not just for this stage, but for generations to come. Maraming maraming salamat po. Thank you to you all. Please welcome us, the Filipino community of Manurewa High School.
