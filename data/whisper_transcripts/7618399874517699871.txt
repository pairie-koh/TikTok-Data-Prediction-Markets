So to the voters of Illinois, if you give Christian Mitchell and me the privilege of leading this state for the next four years, we will adopt a 21st century housing plan that brings home ownership within reach for the next generation. We will bring down electric bills by making Illinois the standard bearer for nuclear and renewable energy production. We will lower the cost of health care by cutting prescription drug costs like we have and continuing to eliminate bills for unexpected health emergencies. We will make Illinois the heart of American innovation in quantum technology. We will accelerate economic growth and job creation by continuing to attract new businesses to our state. We will raise wages while building the skilled workforce of the future.
