Hi, I'm Rebecca Amidon running for the Michigan State Senate in District 32. We've just found out that due to the federal government shutdown caused by the Republicans who hold the majority in both the House and Senate that SNAP benefits, which is food assistance in Michigan, will be discontinued as of November 1st until the government is reopened. Almost half a million children in Michigan depend on SNAP benefits to be able to eat. If you are a person or a family who will be losing SNAP benefits, call your local United Way at 2-1-1 to find a food pantry near you or check out Feeding America West Michigan's website to find the date of when they'll be in your county. This is a call to action for all Michiganders to please donate to your local food pantries. We cannot allow Michiganders to go hungry.
