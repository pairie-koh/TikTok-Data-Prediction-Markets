Could Donald Trump reschedule marijuana now? For context, currently marijuana is a schedule 1 drug, meaning the federal government holds it has no medical use and has a high risk of abuse. Other schedule 1 drugs include heroin, L S D, and ecstasy. According to Cali's prediction market, there's a 61% chance that marijuana will be rescheduled before 2026 and a 77% chance it gets rescheduled before 2027. But why the big shift? Donald Trump has said in a press conference that his administration is looking at rescheduling marijuana and that a decision will be coming in a matter of weeks. According to the Wall Street Journal, the administration is considering dropping marijuana down to a Schedule 3 drug, which are defined as moderate to low potential for physical and psychological dependence. This change would be massive for medical research institutions, as it would make studying the effects of marijuana more possible. It would be great if research institutions weren't defunded, but a win is a win.
