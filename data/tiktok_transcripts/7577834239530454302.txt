Coplan says Polymarket's data can be used to forecast the future. in the run-up to the 2024 presidential election, While pundits opined and pollsters canvassed, Polymarket posted a simple question for users to put money on. Who will be the presidential election winner in 2024? Donald Trump started pulling away in early October. I think one thing that's important to understand about polymarket is it is not a poll.
