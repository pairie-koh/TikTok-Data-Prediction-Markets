Is Donald Trump trying to fire Federal Reserve Chair Jerome Powell? According to Calchi, the chances of Trump firing Powell rose to 22%, which is a 10% increase from just last week. Now, this is all coming as Trump has just held a meeting with House Republicans and asked them whether or not he should fire Powell. The Republicans resoundingly said yes, and Trump said he would follow through, according to White House official. But since then, Donald Trump has said that he is not likely to fire Powell, even though he's reportedly drafted the letter to fire him already. Trump has claimed that he would only fire Powell if there was quote, unquote fraud. Now, here's the interesting thing. As we've seen from the DOGE efforts, this administration has a rather loose definition of what fraud is. Now, if Trump did follow through on his promise to the Republicans, he would be the first president to ever fire a Federal Reserve chair. And this is something that Jerome Powell himself has called illegal. Now, of course, this is just heating up. We'll keep you posted.
