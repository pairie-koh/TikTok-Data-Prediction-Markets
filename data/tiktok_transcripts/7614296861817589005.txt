Prediction markets are bullshit, but serious traders are using them to make lots of money. And it's not by placing bets on Poly market or Kalshi. Prediction markets are an early way to see positioning. You see what the crowd is willing to pay for, for the headline before the data and sometimes before the broader market moves. One example of this was the Iran war. If you look at the data, there were late trades placed on poly market hours before the Iran strikes were announced. In this scenario, the data was so good and so early that the platforms are now under investigation and they're refusing to pay out the winnings. Using the prediction markets as your underlying bet is inherently foolish because of the thin liquidity and the insider bias. I'm watching price first, but prediction markets can confirm or show you that the market is off sides before a huge move. So are prediction markets bullshit? Yes, if you're trading on prediction markets, but they can heavily influence your bets in the real market.
