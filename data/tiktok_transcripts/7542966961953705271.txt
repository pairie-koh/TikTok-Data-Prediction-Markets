Donald Trump Jr. Has invested double digit millions into a crypto platform called Polymarket. Now, for those that don't know, Polymarket is what's called a prediction market. That means instead of buying stocks or coins, you're betting on real world events. Things like will a certain politician win an election, when inflation go up or down, will a company hit its goals or not? People put money on their predictions and if they're right, they profit. Polymarket has become the world's largest platform in the world for this with billions of dollars traded across politics, economics and other major events. Trump Jr. Isn't just throwing money at it, he's also joining their advisory board, which shows he wants to play a bigger role in shaping the platform. And why does this matter? A poly market has recently received a 1 billion dollar valuation, cleaned up past regulatory issues and even acquired a licensed United States exchange so they can operate more openly in the American market. So bottom line, this move is a signal that prediction markets and crypto as a whole are gaining attention from big names and some serious investors.
