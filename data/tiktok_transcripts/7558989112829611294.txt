The Polymarket Paradox. What happens when truth itself becomes a bet on polymarket? People don't just debate reality, they trade it. They profit off predictions. And the more people believe an outcome, the more real it starts to feel. But here's the catch. When truth becomes profitable, manipulation becomes strategy. You don't need to find the truth, just make people believe your version of it. That's the polymarket paradox. When the odds decide reality, honesty doesn't stand a chance.
