One word can make you rich overnight, or the entire prediction market can be manipulated. Today's video must be seen at the end, because it will refresh your understanding of the pony market. Recently, a very outrageous thing happened, an Iranian missile landed in the Israeli open land, and there were no casualties. It was just an ordinary news, but because of a wonderful event in the prediction market, a word in this news was given a value of hundreds of millions of dollars. The thing is, someone opened a prediction event on ponymarket, whether Iran will strike someday has been dabbled, and the judgment rules of this event actually rely on your description of the media report. If the reporter writes that the missile hit, it will be judged as yes, if it writes that there is no missile strike or intercepted debris falling, it will be judged as no. As a result, a group of people who are negative started to send emails to the reporter frantically, and even threatened him to change the script just to change the missile to pieces, because once changed, they can get four to ten times the profit. Seeing this, everyone should realize a core problem. The prediction result of this event does not entirely depend on the real event itself, but on how the information is described. Then you have to talk about a core concept called an oracle. What is an oracle? Simply put, an oracle is a bridge used by the blockchain world to obtain real-world information, because smart contracts themselves cannot directly know what is happening in the real world, so they must feed off-chain data to the chain through oracles, such as price, weather, game results, and even coverage. In the prediction market, the oracle determines the final settlement result, and this is where the problem arises. If the data source of the oracle is affected or even manipulated by humans, the result is no longer fair. In this incident, the reporter actually played the role of a human oracle, and his report was the data source. The players involved did not hire hackers to hack the system or attack the contract, but directly affected the data source itself, changing the content of the report through threat use, thereby affecting the market settlement. This is essentially a new type of oracle attack, which is more terrifying than traditional hackers, because it attacks human nature rather than code. Many people have always believed that the prediction market of the pony market is completely fair and relies on the wisdom of the group. And this incident tells you that as long as there is enough profit motivation, there will definitely be people trying to manipulate the breath source. Once the data of the oracle is unreliable, the results of the entire market will be distorted and even manipulated by humans. What's even more terrifying is that this model will affect the real world instead. It was originally an event that affected the market, but now it has become a market, which in turn affects events and reports, and may even give rise to fake news and public opinion manipulation. In the end, the information you see may have been processed by the interested parties. So here I would like to give a very important reminder to all external business players. First, the prediction market is not absolutely fair, it relies on oracles, which is a risk point in itself. Second, don't blindly believe in so-called probability, because behind this probability may be manipulated information. Third, when you see a prediction event, be sure to consider whether the data source is easy to interfere. If the answer is that it can be artificially influenced, then the market is not essentially predicting the future, but betting on who can manipulate the information more. Remember that the blockchain can achieve code transparency, but it cannot achieve absolute authenticity in the real world. Once the off-chain data is contaminated, the perfect smart contract program is just a machine that executes the wrong results.
