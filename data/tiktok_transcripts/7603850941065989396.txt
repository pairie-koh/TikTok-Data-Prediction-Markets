Kalshi made 14 and a half million dollars for the 2024 presidential election. Essentially how Kalshi works is it lets you buy a $1 contract that executes if a binary event happens. And then they set the price of the $1 contract to make it so everyone who thinks one of the binary options will happen will account for the payment to everyone who thinks the other option will happen, that the cost to say yes or no to an option will always equal $1. And this is all to say that Kalshi doesn't make money when you lose a bet. All their money just goes to the people who bet on the other binary outcome to happen. Instead, Kalshi takes a 2 cent fee on every $1 contract. And they also made $9.6 million from non presidential election based contracts bring them to $24 million where they pay their 224 full time employees $8 million collectively, where most of their payments are obviously in stock options. They spent 6 million on servers, only 4 million on marketing, which is remarkable due to their grassroots free marketing strategy, where with 2 million in legal cost, 2 million to their admin and overhead, they're left with $2 million left over.
