The youngest female billionaire in the world, the co founder of Kalshi, has officially earned that title. Luana Lopes Lara is a Brazilian American entrepreneur who co founded the financial exchange and prediction market at just 21 years old. Before moving into the tech world, she performed as a professional ballerina in Austria and describes her high school ballet training as the most intense years of her life. She met co founder Tarik Mansour at MIT, where they both majored in computer science. The two came up with the billion dollar idea one day while walking home from their internship.
