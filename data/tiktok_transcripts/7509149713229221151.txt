A cancer scare for former President Joe Biden. The 46th president was diagnosed Friday with prostate cancer that's metastasized to his bones, according to a statement from the former president's office. The statement says, quote, while this represents a more aggressive form of the disease, the cancer appears to be hormone sensitive, which allows for effective management. End quote. The 82 year old Biden and his family are reviewing treatment options with doctors, the statement concludes.
