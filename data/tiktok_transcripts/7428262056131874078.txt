Betting markets could change elections forever. Millions of dollars worth of bets give Trump a 16 point lead on Harris right now, according to Kalshi. That's a way wider margin than most mainstream polls. Question is whether betting markets are more or less reliable than traditional surveys where opinions are free. In theory, betting markets are more honest because people put real money on the line and skin in the game should make opinions more concrete. But critics say betting markets can be manipulated by super sized bets. But if prediction markets like Kalshi end up being right in November, could Mark the beginning of the end for traditional polls. More people will start paying attention to where money is going instead of what anonymous surveys are saying. For now it's anyone's guess. What do you trust more at this point?
